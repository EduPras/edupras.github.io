I am a Machine Learning Engineer and Computer Engineering student (graduating in late 2026) from the Federal University of Technology - Paraná (UTFPR), Brazil. My research focuses on efficient model adaptation and geospatial foundation models for environmental monitoring. I am an affiliated researcher at [CEIA-UFG](https://ceia.ufg.br/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, one of the largest AI research centers in the world, where I bridge cutting-edge AI research and industry applications.

I have been awarded the [MITACS](https://www.mitacs.ca/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} Globalink Research Award twice. The first internship took me to [INRS](https://inrs.ca/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in Montréal, where I worked with [Prof. Leszek Szczecinski](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=elDOrQIAAAAJ&hl=en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} on parameter-efficient fine-tuning for vision-language models (CLIP-LoRA-XS). The second brought me to [Université Laval](https://www.ulaval.ca/en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} in Québec City. At the IMOM lab, under the supervision of [Prof. Chengkai Fan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qsHbxsMAAAAJ&hl=en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, we apply machine learning algorithms in sustainable mining engineering.

My capstone project applied NASA and IBM's Prithvi-EO-2.0 foundation model to detect endangered *araucaria angustifolia* canopy from satellite imagery, supervised by [Prof. Daniel Cavalcanti](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=owEu8FkAAAAJ&hl=en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} at UTFPR. I have also published work on IoT platform scalability at WPerformance 2025.

## Research Interests
Edge AI · Foundation Models · Environmental Applications · Embedded Systems
